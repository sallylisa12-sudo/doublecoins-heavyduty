Financing FAQ

Is there a minimum purchase amount for financing?

PayPal Pay in 4 is available on orders between $30 and $1,500. Katapult has no minimum purchase amount for their lease-to-own program.

Will applying affect my credit score?

PayPal Pay in 4 uses a soft credit check that does not affect your credit score. Katapult's lease-to-own program does not require a credit check at all.

How quickly can I get my tires if I finance?

Financing approvals are typically instant. Once approved, your order ships within 1-2 business days, the same as any other order.

Can I return tires purchased with financing?

Yes. Our standard 45-day return policy applies to all purchases, including financed orders. The refund will be processed back through your financing provider.
