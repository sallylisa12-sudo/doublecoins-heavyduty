About Tires
What's the difference between steer, drive, and trailer tires? +
Steer tires go on the front axle and are designed for excellent handling, steering response, and even wear. Drive tires go on the rear powered axles and feature deeper treads for better traction. Trailer tires are designed for lower rolling resistance and stability under load.
How do I read a tire size? +
For example, 295/75R22.5: 295 is the width in mm, 75 is the aspect ratio (height as % of width), R means radial, and 22.5 is the rim diameter in inches. For sizes like 11R22.5, 11 is the width in inches.
What does SmartWay certified mean? +
SmartWay is an EPA program that certifies tires meeting low rolling resistance standards. SmartWay certified tires can reduce fuel consumption by up to 3%, saving you money on long haul operations.
What is a retreadable casing? +
A retreadable casing means the tire body can be reused with new tread applied after the original tread wears out. This significantly reduces cost per mile and is common in commercial truck operations.
