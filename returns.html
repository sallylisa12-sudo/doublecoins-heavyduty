Return Policy

We stand behind every tire we sell

45-Day Return Policy: We offer a 45-day return window on all tires purchased from Double Coin Heavy Duty Truck Warehouse. Your satisfaction is our priority.

1Eligibility

To be eligible for a return, the following conditions must be met:

  • Tires must be unused, unmounted, and in original condition
  • Return request must be made within 45 days of the delivery date
  • Tires must not show signs of road use, mounting damage, or improper storage
  • Original packaging should be intact when possible

2How to Initiate a Return

Starting a return is simple:

  • Contact our team by phone at (718) 431-5752 or via our contact form
  • Provide your order number and the reason for your return
  • Our team will review your request and provide return instructions within 1 business day
  • Once approved, you will receive a return shipping label or pickup instructions

3Refund Process

Once we receive and inspect the returned tires:

  • Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days of receiving the returned items
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method
  • You will receive an email confirmation once your refund has been processed

4Exchanges

Need a different size or model? We're happy to help with exchanges:

  • Contact us to arrange an exchange for a different tire
  • If the replacement tire is a different price, we will adjust the difference accordingly
  • Exchange shipping is handled on a case-by-case basis

5Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned:

  • Tires that have been mounted, driven on, or show signs of use
  • Tires with sidewall damage caused after delivery
  • Special-order or custom-order tires (unless defective)

6Damaged or Defective Tires

If you receive damaged or defective tires:

  • Contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the damage
  • We will arrange a free replacement or full refund at no extra cost to you
  • Do not mount or use damaged tires — contact us first for instructions

Questions about returns?

Our team is here to help with any return or exchange questions.
